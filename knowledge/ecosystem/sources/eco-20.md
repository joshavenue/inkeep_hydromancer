# Ergonia

Ergonia is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: market maker/infra.

## What it is

Ergonia describes itself on X as a DeFi trading desk and venture studio that does onchain market making, DEX liquidity provision, and early-stage company building. Its stated focus areas include DeFi, infrastructure, yield, perps, and consumer crypto. The ergonia.io website currently redirects to a DRW Labs page.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- fills history per wallet
- funding rates
- live positions for own accounts
