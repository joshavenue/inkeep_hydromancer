# Polymarket

Polymarket is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: prediction markets.

## What it is

Polymarket is a prediction market where users trade on the outcomes of events in politics, sports, crypto, culture, and other topics. US users are directed to a separate site, polymarket.us. It is not built on Hyperliquid.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Polymarket announced Hyperliquid deposits and withdrawals in October 2025 and lists markets about Hyperliquid and HYPE, but it does not run on Hyperliquid.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- deposit and withdrawal status on Hyperliquid
- HYPE spot price for market resolution
- Hyperliquid protocol metrics for event markets
- HIP-4 prediction market data for comparison
