# Iceberg

Iceberg is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Iceberg is an on-chain crypto prop trading firm that gives traders up to $100,000 of simulated capital and an 80% share of funded profits. It offers Instant Funding accounts with no evaluation and 1-Step Unlimited accounts with a 10% profit target, both with 3% daily and 6% total drawdown limits. Payouts come from a public payout pool funded by account fees, and it runs an affiliate program and Ice Points rewards.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

The website states Iceberg is powered by Hyperliquid, and its X account describes it as built on Hyperliquid perps.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- real-time mark prices and order book stream
- candles
- funding rates
- fills and positions per trader account
- perp and HIP-3 market metadata
