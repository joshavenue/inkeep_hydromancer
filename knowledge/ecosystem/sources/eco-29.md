# Hunch

Hunch is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: prediction markets.

## What it is

Hunch is a trading terminal and aggregator for prediction markets that combines market discovery, wallet tracking, cross-venue price comparison, and execution in one interface. It also offers a Telegram bot and a points, cashback, and referral rewards program. It is used by prediction market traders focused on sports, politics, and news events.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Its website lists Hyperliquid as one of its supported prediction market venues, alongside Polymarket, Kalshi, Limitless, Opinion, and Melee.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- prediction/outcome market metadata
- order book stream for outcome markets
- live prices and odds changes
- fills history per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
