# Wasabi

Wasabi is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Wasabi Protocol is a leverage trading protocol that offers up to 20x leverage on hundreds of assets, including long-tail tokens, memecoins, and tokenized stocks. Users can also deposit into vaults that earn yield from trading activity. It operates on Ethereum mainnet, Base, Solana, and other chains.
