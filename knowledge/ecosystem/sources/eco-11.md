# Cielo

Cielo is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: analytics/dashboard.

## What it is

Cielo is a wallet tracking and on-chain analytics platform covering 30+ blockchains, with wallet profiles showing PnL and trade history, wallet discovery, and real-time alerts via Telegram and Discord. It also offers a trading terminal with built-in Solana swaps and copy-trading agents. Its users are active on-chain traders who follow and copy high-performing wallets.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Cielo lists Hyperliquid as a supported network for wallet tracking and wallet intelligence alongside EVM chains and Solana.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- fills history per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
- real-time trade stream for tracked wallets (alerts)
- PnL and win-rate history per wallet
- liquidation feed
