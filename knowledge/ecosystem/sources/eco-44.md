# Mullet

Mullet is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Mullet is a mobile trading app for leveraged contracts on forex, gold, crude oil, stock indices, and crypto, with 59 instruments across five asset classes. Its website says orders execute off-chain while liquidations, settlements, and quotes are recorded on Solana. It offers a demo account, negative balance protection, and custody without private key management.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- reference prices and candles for TradFi-linked perps (HIP-3 markets)
- order book stream
- funding rates
- oracle prices
