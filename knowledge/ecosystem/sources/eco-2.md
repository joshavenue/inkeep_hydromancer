# AssetDash

AssetDash is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

AssetDash is a portfolio tracking and trading company whose current app, Moby, shows live order flow and whale positioning and lets users trade with one tap. Moby runs on Solana, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, and Base and includes copy trading and rewards. The given domain redirects to moby.win.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

AssetDash's X posts mention adding Hyperliquid spot asset tracking and a Whale Watch Perps feed; Hyperliquid is not listed among Moby's supported chains.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- spot balances per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
- large trade and whale position feed
- liquidation feed
- market metadata
