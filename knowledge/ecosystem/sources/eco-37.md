# Invo

Invo is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: copy/social trading.

## What it is

Invo is a social trading app where users browse leaderboards and trader profiles, copy other traders' positions with one click, and earn when others copy their trades. It includes a social feed, track records for traders, and an 'Invo Score' that rates trader profitability and risk. It is available on web, iOS and Android (app listings use the name Involio), and the site cites 250K+ users.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Its X account posts Hyperliquid market and news updates, such as Hyperliquid liquidation totals, but the website does not state which venue executes trades.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- PnL and trade history per wallet for leaderboards and scoring
- real-time fills stream for copied traders
- liquidation feed
- mark prices and candles
