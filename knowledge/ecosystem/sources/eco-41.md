# Liquid

Liquid is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Liquid is a trading app on web, iOS, and Android for trading perpetuals on crypto, stocks, FX, and commodities 24/7 with up to 50x leverage. It also offers Co-Invest, a connector that lets users trade from ChatGPT or Claude. The given domain redirects to liquid.trade.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Liquid's app routes trades to Hyperliquid markets, including core perps and HIP-3 markets listed under the xyz: prefix.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live prices and candles across core and HIP-3 markets
- order book stream
- HIP-3 market metadata
- live positions and margin per wallet
- fills history per wallet
- funding rates
