# Trasia

Trasia is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Trasia is an Asia-first trading platform with web and mobile apps that serves mainly Chinese-speaking traders. It offers perps trading, TWAP and grid orders, and a beta Scout Mode that tracks whale positions, win rates, and PnL. It also runs community trading competitions.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Trasia describes itself as built on Hyperliquid and lists Hyperliquid perps, including HIP-3 markets such as xyz:SP500.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets (whale tracking)
- fills history per wallet
- order book stream and trades
- candles
- HIP-3 market metadata
- funding rates
