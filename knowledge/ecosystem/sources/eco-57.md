# Sorcery

Sorcery is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Sorcery's website describes it as 'Powering Financial Content' and says it aims to bring any asset to traders and stream liquidity 24/7 in anything that ticks. The site gives no further product details.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- oracle and mark prices
- order book stream
- HIP-3 market metadata
