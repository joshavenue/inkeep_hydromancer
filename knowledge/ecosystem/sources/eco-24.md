# Ferm

Ferm is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Ferm is a perps prop firm where traders pay for a one-step evaluation challenge and, if they pass, receive a simulated funded account with up to a 90% profit split. It lists 84 markets including crypto perpetuals and forex, index, and commodity CFDs, with web and mobile apps. Its site states that challenge and funded orders are synthetic and are not routed to an external market.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- mark and oracle prices for crypto perps
- candles
- order book snapshots for simulated fills
- funding rates
