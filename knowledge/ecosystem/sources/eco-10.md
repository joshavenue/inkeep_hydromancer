# Cabals

Cabals is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: copy/social trading.

## What it is

Cabals is a web trading app with markets, intel, feed, news, and leaderboard sections, plus group chats for named trading groups called cabals. Users connect a wallet through Privy.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Cabals lists Hyperliquid perps, including HIP-3 markets such as xyz:SP500 and xyz:NVDA, using Hyperliquid market data.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- market list with 24h volume
- HIP-3 market metadata
- live positions for many wallets
- PnL per wallet for leaderboards
- candles
- order book stream
