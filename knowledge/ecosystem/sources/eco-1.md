# Arena

Arena is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: copy/social trading.

## What it is

The Arena is a SocialFi app that combines a social feed, tipping, communities, a token launchpad, and a non-custodial wallet with an ARENA token. It has added in-app perps trading with leaderboards, points, and trade sharing. Users include crypto traders, creators, and memecoin communities.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Arena uses Hyperliquid as the engine for its in-app perps trading across 200+ markets, including HIP-3 stock and commodity markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- liquidation feed
- PnL per wallet for leaderboards
- HIP-3 market metadata
- candles
