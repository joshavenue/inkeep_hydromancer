# GTR

GTR is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: copy/social trading.

## What it is

GTR is a social trading platform for crypto perps, stock perps, memecoins, and prediction markets on web and mobile. It combines a trading terminal with live social trade feeds, one-click copy trading of top traders, news and listing alerts, and group trading circles called Clans with leaderboards. It is non-custodial and in beta.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

GTR's website states that it is powered by Hyperliquid.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet for social feeds and copying
- real-time trade stream for followed traders
- PnL and leaderboard data per wallet
- order book stream and candles
- HIP-3 market metadata for stock perps
