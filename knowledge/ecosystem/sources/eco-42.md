# Mass

Mass is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Mass is a self-custodial mobile money app for iOS and Android that combines perpetuals trading, tokenized stocks, prediction markets, savings and payments. It requires no KYC to trade, is available in more than 170 countries, and says its smart contracts were audited by Pashov and Trail of Bits. Its X posts say prediction markets are powered by Polymarket and tokenized stocks by Dinari.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Mass's X posts say it moved its perps product to Hyperliquid in mid-2025 and operates as a Hyperliquid builder frontend for mobile perps trading.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- positions, balances and fills per user wallet
- candles and mark prices
- funding rates
- HIP-3 market metadata
- trade history per wallet
