# Outcome

Outcome is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: prediction markets.

## What it is

Outcome is a prediction markets app where users trade event contracts on sports, crypto, esports, economics, finance, and politics. It runs a trader rewards program and restricts trading in some regions, including the US. X posts describe it as the leading frontend and deployer for prediction markets on Hyperliquid.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Outcome deploys and offers trading in Hyperliquid-native outcome (HIP-4) markets that settle on Hyperliquid's order book, according to posts on X.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-4 outcome market metadata and resolution status
- order book stream per outcome market
- live prices and volume per market
- fills history per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
- deployer and market listing events
