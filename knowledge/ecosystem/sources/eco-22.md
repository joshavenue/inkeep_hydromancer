# Fatbot

Fatbot is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: telegram/trading bot.

## What it is

FatBot is a web-based crypto trading platform focused on token sniping, smart money alerts, whale tracking, copy trading, and perpetual trading with up to 40x leverage. It is tied to the FATTY token and runs volume airdrop and multi-level referral programs. Its users are mainly memecoin traders and FATTY holders.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

FatBot's perps feature trades on Hyperliquid, including HIP-3 assets such as oil, gold, and silver.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions per wallet
- fills history per wallet
- HIP-3 market metadata
- candles
- order book stream
- liquidation feed
