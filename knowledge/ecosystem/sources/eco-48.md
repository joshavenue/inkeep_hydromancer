# OrderX

OrderX is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading terminal.

## What it is

OrderX is an AI trading terminal for stocks, options, perps, and crypto that users drive with natural-language requests for research, positioning, and execution. It publishes options market analysis such as dealer gamma and skew, and supports agentic execution with human approval and configurable guardrails.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

OrderX's website shows Hyperliquid perps as a supported market, including an example strategy that captures spreads and funding between SK Hynix on Hyperliquid and KRX.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- funding rates
- HIP-3 market metadata for equity perps
- candles
- user positions, orders, and fills
