# Faro

Faro is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading terminal.

## What it is

Faro is an AI trading platform built for Hyperliquid that combines onchain data, sentiment, social activity, and other market signals to surface trade ideas. Its app includes charts, an order book, an AI assistant, and order entry for instant execution. It is non-custodial and part of the Swell ecosystem.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Faro is built for Hyperliquid, using its market data and executing trades on it from a new or imported wallet.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- funding rates and open interest
- liquidation feed
- aggregate positioning across many wallets
- live positions per wallet
