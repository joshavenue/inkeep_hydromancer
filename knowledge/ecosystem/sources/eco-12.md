# Click

Click is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading terminal.

## What it is

Click is a non-custodial web trading terminal that combines spot trading on Solana, BNB Chain, Base, and Robinhood Chain with perpetuals and prediction markets from one balance. It includes a token discovery feed, a monitor page, social trending data, copy trading, and cashback rewards. It is in early access with a waitlist, and a mobile app is in private testing.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- perp market metadata including HIP-3 markets
- liquidation feed
- fills history per wallet for copy trading
- user positions and PnL
