# WhaleDance

WhaleDance is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

WhaleDance is a trading app built around AI agents, available on iOS, Android, and the web. Users can ask an AI assistant called Max about trades, and can delegate funds to AI agents that monitor markets 24/7 and trade for them. Its website lists 24/7 markets on stocks, commodities, and crypto with up to 40x leverage.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live prices and candles across crypto, stock, and commodity markets
- order book stream for execution
- live positions and PnL per user and per agent
- fills history per wallet
- funding rates and open interest for agent signals
