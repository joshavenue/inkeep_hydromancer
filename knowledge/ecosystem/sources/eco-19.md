# Entropy

Entropy is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: HIP-3 venue/deployer.

## What it is

Entropy lists perpetual futures on frontier assets such as pre-IPO companies (for example Anthropic and OpenAI) and some equities, and runs its own trading frontend. It runs a referral and rebate program that pays HYPE to users who connect a wallet on its site. Its posts describe a team with backgrounds at trading firms and a funding round led by Ribbit Capital.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Entropy is a HIP-3 deployer whose markets settle on Hyperliquid and are also tradable through other Hyperliquid frontends such as Trust Wallet.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-3 market metadata
- open interest per market
- volume and fees per deployer
- fills history per wallet for rebates
- oracle and mark prices
- order book stream
