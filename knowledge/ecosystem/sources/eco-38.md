# Kinetiq

Kinetiq is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: HIP-3 venue/deployer.

## What it is

Kinetiq is a liquid staking protocol on Hyperliquid where users stake HYPE and receive kHYPE, which earns staking rewards and is used across Hyperliquid DeFi protocols. It also runs Markets.xyz, a venue for 24/7 perpetual futures on equities, indices, currencies, and commodities. Its Launch product lets stakers back HIP-3 deployers and earn a share of their trading fees.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Kinetiq stakes HYPE with Hyperliquid validators to issue kHYPE and operates Markets.xyz, a perpetuals venue powered by Hyperliquid HIP-3.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- validator and staking state
- HIP-3 market metadata and deployer state
- order book stream and candles for its markets
- open interest and volume per market
- funding rates
- oracle prices for HIP-3 markets
