# Bullpen

Bullpen is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Bullpen is a non-custodial trading app that combines Solana tokens, perps, and Polymarket prediction markets in one interface with social and competition features. It supports deposits and withdrawals via same-day ACH and uses Turnkey for key management. It was co-founded by crypto trader Ansem.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Bullpen routes perp and spot trades to Hyperliquid and shows Hyperliquid insights such as net positioning, open interest, and volatility.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- open interest and net positioning data
- user positions, orders, and fills
- funding rates
- HIP-3 market metadata
