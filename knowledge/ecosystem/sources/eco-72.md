# Wick

Wick is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Wick is a mobile trading app for iOS and Android that combines perpetuals with prediction markets, including a sportsbook-style interface for sports events. It offers position management, market movers, leaderboards, new-market alerts, and Privy-secured wallets with no KYC.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Wick's website says users can trade Hyperliquid and Polymarket markets in one app.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- new market listings and HIP-3 market metadata
- user positions, orders, and fills
- PnL per wallet for leaderboards
