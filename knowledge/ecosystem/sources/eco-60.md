# Talis

Talis is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Talis is an AI trading copilot app on web, iOS, and Android for trading crypto, stocks, commodities, prediction markets, and pre-IPO markets in one portfolio. It offers AI research, copy trading, paper trading, backtesting, natural-language automated strategies, and sub-account management. Trades are placed through a delegated agent wallet that can sign orders but cannot withdraw funds.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Talis executes trades on Hyperliquid, where each user's balance sits in their own Hyperliquid account.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet for copy trading
- historical candles for backtesting
- order book stream
- HIP-3 market metadata
- sub-account and agent wallet state
