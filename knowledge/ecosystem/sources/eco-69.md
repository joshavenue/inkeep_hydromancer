# Vigil Labs

Vigil Labs is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Vigil Labs is an applied research lab in Palo Alto building machine intelligence systems for live markets. It starts by helping human traders detect, surface, and forecast market-relevant events, with a stated long-term goal of building a prediction engine. Its team lists backgrounds at Citadel Securities, xAI, Millennium, and prediction market trading.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- trade and order book streams across markets
- candles
- funding rates and open interest
- liquidation feed
- large-wallet position changes
