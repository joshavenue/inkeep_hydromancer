# Ult

Ult is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Ult is a self-custody trading app from Cronos Labs for crypto, stock perps, commodities, sports, and prediction markets on iPhone, Android, and desktop. It started as the Crypto.com Onchain Wallet and was renamed Ult in 2026. The given domain redirects to cronos.com.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live prices and candles across perp markets
- order book stream
- live positions per wallet
- fills history per wallet
- market metadata for stock and commodity perps
