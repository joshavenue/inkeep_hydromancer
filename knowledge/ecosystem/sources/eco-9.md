# Button

Button is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Button makes Tailor, an AI trading assistant app for trading stocks, crypto, and other markets. Tailor researches markets, reads the user's portfolio and open orders, drafts trades that the user confirms, and runs scheduled briefs and price alerts by push notification or Telegram. Access is through a waitlist.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

The website says users can look up any trader on Hyperliquid, see their account and holdings, and get notified when followed traders open or close a trade.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- open orders per wallet
- candles
- price feed for alerts
- HIP-3 market metadata
