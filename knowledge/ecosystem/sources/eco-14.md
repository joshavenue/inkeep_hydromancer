# Delpho

Delpho is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Delpho issues USDV, a yield-bearing dollar on HyperEVM that users mint at 0% interest against HYPE, kHYPE, USDC or USDT collateral. Staking USDV into sUSDV earns yield from a delta-neutral position whose short leg collects funding paid by leveraged longs. The protocol has a Sherlock audit, and USDV is designed for use across HyperEVM lending, liquidity and trading venues.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Delpho describes itself as Hyperliquid's balance-sheet layer; deposited collateral runs a delta-neutral position on Hyperliquid, and USDV lives on HyperEVM.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- funding rates and funding history
- protocol vault positions and margin
- mark and oracle prices for HYPE and collateral assets
- HyperEVM collateral and supply state
- HyperCore and HyperEVM transfer events
