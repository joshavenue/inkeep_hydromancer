# GTE

GTE is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

GTE describes itself as a fully on-chain order book exchange with centralized-exchange latency, offering perps, spot, and an API built for agents. It includes an AI trading agent called EDGE that turns market context into trade ideas. Access is rolling out in cohorts from a large waitlist.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

GTE's X account has listed HIP-4 support among its product updates, but how its exchange connects to Hyperliquid is not detailed publicly.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- prediction or outcome market metadata (HIP-4)
- user positions, orders, and fills
- funding rates
