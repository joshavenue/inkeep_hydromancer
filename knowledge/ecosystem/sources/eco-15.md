# Dexari

Dexari is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Dexari is a self-custodial mobile app for iOS and Android for trading perps and spot markets with up to 40x leverage, TradingView charts, and advanced order types such as TP/SL and TWAP. It uses Turnkey for self-custody and supports deposits by card, bank transfer, or crypto. It targets traders who want to trade onchain markets from their phone.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Dexari states it is powered by Hyperliquid's order book and gives mobile access to Hyperliquid perps, spot, and HIP-3 markets such as stocks and commodities.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- live positions and margin per wallet
- order fill notifications per wallet
- HIP-3 market metadata
- new listing announcements
