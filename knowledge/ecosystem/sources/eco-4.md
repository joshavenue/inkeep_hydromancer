# Auto

Auto is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: telegram/trading bot.

## What it is

Auto is an AI agent platform for onchain finance that lets users have AI agents retrieve market data and execute trades across chains. It grew out of the auto.fun platform from the elizaOS ecosystem.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

X posts mention bridging to HyperEVM; no deeper Hyperliquid integration is stated publicly.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live prices and candles
- order book stream
- live positions per wallet
- fills history per wallet
- HyperEVM bridge and balance data

## Status

appears inactive or shut down (public evidence: in September 2026 the team posted on X that it had shut down all production after its account was hacked, and auto.fun returned a Cloudflare 525 error when checked)
