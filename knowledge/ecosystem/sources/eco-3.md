# Aura

Aura is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Aura is a trading app on iOS, Android, and desktop for trading Hyperliquid and Polymarket markets from one wallet. Users can sign up for an Aura account or bring their own wallet. It includes perps and spot trading, social groups, missions, and points.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Aura offers Hyperliquid perps, spot, and HIP-3 markets through its app, and its X posts describe launching HIP-3 markets such as US Treasury perps.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live prices and candles across core and HIP-3 markets
- order book stream
- HIP-3 market metadata
- live positions per wallet
- fills history per wallet
- trading volume per user for missions
