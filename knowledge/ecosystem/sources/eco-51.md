# Propr

Propr is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Propr is a perps proprietary trading firm where traders pass an evaluation challenge to get a funded account and receive a share of rewards paid onchain in USDC. It covers 160+ crypto, stock, index, commodity, and forex perps and publishes payouts on a transparency page. It is API-first, with REST and SDK access, and allows algorithmic and agent trading.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Propr describes itself as the perps prop firm built on Hyperliquid, offering Hyperliquid perps including HIP-3 markets; its site also lists Lighter.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- PnL and drawdown per account
- HIP-3 market metadata
- mark prices
- order book stream
