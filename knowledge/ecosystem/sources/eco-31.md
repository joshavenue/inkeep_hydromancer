# Hypemarkets

Hypemarkets is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: analytics/dashboard.

## What it is

Hype Markets is a Hyperliquid market intelligence site with tables of perps, spot, and prediction markets showing price, volume, open interest, and funding. It also offers screeners, whale and wallet tracking, and market briefs, and lets users trade from market pages.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Hype Markets displays and links to trading on Hyperliquid perps, spot, HIP-3, and outcome markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- market metadata for all perps, including HIP-3
- funding rates and open interest
- 24h volume and trade counts
- live positions for many wallets
- order book stream
- HIP-4 outcome market data
