# Duels

Duels is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Duels is a gamified trading platform where individuals or verified teams compete trading perpetuals on 100+ assets. It features leaderboards, an AURA points system and instant USDC payouts, and presents trading as an esport. The website is titled 'Duels | Gamified Trading' but shows little text without loading the app.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Its X posts reference Hyperliquid as the venue for the 100+ perp markets traded in its competitions.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- fills history per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
- PnL per wallet for leaderboards
- mark prices and candles
- order book stream
