# Legend Trade

Legend Trade is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: copy/social trading.

## What it is

Legend is a social onchain trading app for crypto, stocks, and commodities, available on web, iOS, and Android. Trader profiles show public trades and PnL, and users can copy other traders, join leaderboards, and compete in seasonal competitions. It is used by perp traders who want social discovery and copy trading.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

It is a frontend for Hyperliquid perps, and its market list includes HIP-3 markets such as xyz:SP500, xyz:NVDA, and xyz:SILVER alongside native Hyperliquid perps like HYPE.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- PnL and leaderboard data per wallet
- HIP-3 market metadata
- order book stream
- candles
