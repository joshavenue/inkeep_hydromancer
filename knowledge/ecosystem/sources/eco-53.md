# Senpi

Senpi is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: telegram/trading bot.

## What it is

Senpi is an AI trading agent app that analyzes a wallet's trade history, scores trading habits, and builds and runs automated strategies around the clock. It offers more than 100 strategy templates, a plain-English strategy builder, a Chrome extension, and iOS and Android apps. It also runs agent trading competitions.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Senpi is built for Hyperliquid: it reads any Hyperliquid address and executes its agents' strategies on Hyperliquid markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- fills history per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
- top trader positioning
- candles
- funding rates
- order book stream
