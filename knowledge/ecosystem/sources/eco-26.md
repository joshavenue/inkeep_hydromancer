# Force

Force is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: HIP-3 venue/deployer.

## What it is

Force offers macro indices, such as a US 10-year Treasury index (US10Y), as onchain tradable markets. Its website gives little further detail.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Force says its macro indices are live on Hyperliquid, and its trade link points to a frce:US10Y market on Hyperliquid testnet.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-3 market metadata
- oracle and mark prices
- order book stream
- open interest per market
- candles
