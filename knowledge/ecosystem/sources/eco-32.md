# Hypercall

Hypercall is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Hypercall is an onchain options exchange for crypto and tokenized equities, offering calls, puts, and multi-leg strategies with no minimum size and no liquidations on long options. It is built by Synapse Protocol and runs on desktop and mobile, 24/7 including weekends. It also offers a liquidity vault (SLP) that quotes options and hedges positions.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Options settle on Hyperliquid, and its vault hedges using Hyperliquid perps, spot, and HIP-3 equity perps.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- oracle and mark prices for underlyings
- order book stream
- HIP-3 market metadata
- funding rates
- candles
- spot balances and positions for hedging accounts
