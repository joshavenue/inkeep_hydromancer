# HyENA

HyENA is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: HIP-3 venue/deployer.

## What it is

HyENA is a perpetuals exchange margined in Ethena's USDe, launched by Based. Traders earn rewards on their USDe margin while holding positions, and liquidity providers can deposit into an HLPe vault. It has its own web trading app and runs a points program.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

HyENA is a HIP-3 perp venue deployed on Hyperliquid, and its markets can also be traded on Hyperliquid's own frontend.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-3 market metadata for its deployed markets
- order book stream
- live positions and margin for many wallets
- fills history per wallet for points
- liquidation feed
- vault equity and PnL history
