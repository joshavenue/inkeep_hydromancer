# Based

Based is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Based is a self-custodial trading app for crypto perpetuals and US stock markets, available as a web terminal, desktop app, iOS and Android apps, and a Telegram trading bot. It also offers Based Cash, a Visa debit card for spending crypto, plus multichain deposits, prediction market access, and Based Cloud, a tool for launching branded trading terminals. The site reports more than $44 billion in volume and 100k+ users.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Based is built on Hyperliquid and routes trading to Hyperliquid perps and HIP-3 equity markets (its stock pages use the xyz: market prefix), and its X posts describe support for HIP-4 outcome markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles and mark prices
- positions, balances and fills per user wallet
- HIP-3 market metadata
- funding rates
- HIP-4 outcome market data
