# Native Markets

Native Markets is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Native Markets is the issuer of USDH, a stablecoin built for Hyperliquid and minted on HyperEVM with a USDH/USDC spot order book on HyperCore. USDH was used for spot trading, portfolio margin and as the quote asset for USDH-denominated markets, including HIP-3 perps. Its website describes the company as building payments, risk and yield products, with a team from BlackRock, Uniswap, Stripe, Circle and Scale AI.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Native Markets issued USDH as a Hyperliquid-native stablecoin and staked HYPE to give USDH-quoted markets aligned quote asset fee benefits.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- spot order book for USDH/USDC
- spot balances and transfers per wallet
- HIP-3 market metadata for USDH-quoted markets
- staking and delegation state
- portfolio margin borrow and supply state

## Status

appears inactive or shut down (public evidence: @nativemarkets X posts announce a USDH sunset, including unstaking HYPE, removing some USDH spot markets and directing users to convert to USDC)
