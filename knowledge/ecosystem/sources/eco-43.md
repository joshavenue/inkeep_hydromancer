# Monarch

Monarch is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: market maker/infra.

## What it is

Monarch builds liquidity tooling for new and long-tail markets, centered on Marina, a program that pays makers on-chain rewards based on quoting, spreads, depth and fills. Its site lists Outcome.xyz liquidity reward campaigns across sports, macro and price-target markets, with a wallet leaderboard and per-market stats. It also offers Marina Earn vaults that run on-chain market-making strategies and helps projects launch their own markets.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Monarch runs liquidity incentives for markets on Hyperliquid, including Outcome.xyz HIP-4 markets and spot listings, and offers help launching HIP-3 and HIP-4 markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book snapshots per block
- open orders and quotes per wallet
- maker and taker fills per wallet
- HIP-4 outcome market metadata
- HIP-3 market metadata
- spread and depth history per market
