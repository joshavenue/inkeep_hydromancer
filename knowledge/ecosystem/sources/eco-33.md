# Hyperdash

Hyperdash is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading terminal.

## What it is

Hyperdash, per its X account, is a trading terminal and analytics suite for Hyperliquid with advanced order types such as iceberg and VWAP orders, a flow inspector, and trader and address pages with leaderboards. The website listed for this entry, pvp.trade, is a Telegram bot that lets groups long and short tokens together and copy or countertrade friends. It is used by active Hyperliquid traders who want execution tools and onchain flow visibility.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Hyperdash builds its trading and analytics on Hyperliquid perps and onchain activity, and pvp.trade states it is powered by Hyperliquid.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- trades stream with wallet attribution
- order book stream
- candles
- liquidation feed
