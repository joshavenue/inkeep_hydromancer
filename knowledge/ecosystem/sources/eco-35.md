# Hypernova

Hypernova is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Hypernova is an on-chain crypto prop trading firm that funds traders who pass an assessment, with accounts from $5k to $200k and up to $300k total initial funding. Traders keep 80% of profits (90% with an add-on), and payouts settle from a public on-chain reserve in seconds. It offers 106 perpetual markets across crypto, equities, commodities and pre-IPO names, and is in an invite-only closed beta.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Hypernova is built on Hyperliquid, prices its markets from live Hyperliquid market data, and lets traders connect Hyperliquid trading history for assessments.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- real-time mark prices and order book stream
- candles
- fills history per wallet
- funding rates
- HIP-3 market metadata
- PnL history per trader wallet
