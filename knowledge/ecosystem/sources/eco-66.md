# Valantis

Valantis is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Valantis runs stHYPE, a liquid staking token for HYPE, along with the stHYPE AMM, a pool built for liquid staking tokens that earns swap, lending and staking yield. Its Prime Trading product gives stHYPE holders up to 40% off trading fees when trading through a Prime account, and it also offers Prime DCA. Its site says stHYPE yield comes from staking plus payments from HIP-3 and stablecoin businesses on Hyperliquid.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Valantis is built on Hyperliquid and HyperEVM; Prime Trading discounts apply across Hyperliquid perps, spot and HIP-3 markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- staking and delegation state
- user fee tiers and 14-day trading volume
- fills history per wallet
- HIP-3 market metadata
- spot and perp order book stream
- HyperCore and HyperEVM transfer events
