# Bagel

Bagel is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: prediction markets.

## What it is

Bagel is a mobile app for iOS and Android that offers a simplified interface for trading Polymarket prediction markets on sports, esports, crypto, and trending events. It adds copy trading, combo bets, in-app chat, and a non-custodial wallet through Privy, with multi-language support aimed at users outside the US.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- prediction market outcome prices (if HIP-4 outcome markets are added)
- fills history per wallet for copy trading
- live positions for many wallets
- market metadata and settlement status
