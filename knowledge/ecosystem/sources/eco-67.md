# Valiant

Valiant is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

Valiant is a trading platform on the Fogo blockchain, with a spot swap interface (for example USDC to FOGO) and liquidity provision tied to Fogo ecosystem points and badges. It previously offered perpetuals, outcome markets and an AI trading agent called Maximus, but its X account announced a wind-down of Valiant Perps and Outcomes. It runs on Fogo, not on Hyperliquid.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Its X posts promoted trading 'the exact Hyperliquid liquidity' on Fogo for its perps product, which has since been wound down.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- mark prices and candles
- funding rates
- perp market metadata
