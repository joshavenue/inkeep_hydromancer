# Dapital

Dapital is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: copy/social trading.

## What it is

Dapital describes itself as a financialized social network where users post trade ideas and discussions and can trade directly from the feed. It offers perps, crypto, and pre-IPO markets through iOS and Android apps. Users include traders and creators who share calls and theses.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Public posts describe its perp trades as executing on Hyperliquid using Hyperliquid builder codes.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- live positions for many wallets
- fills history per wallet
- PnL per wallet
- order book stream
- candles
- HIP-3 market metadata
