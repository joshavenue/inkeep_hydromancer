# Felix Protocol

Felix Protocol is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: HIP-3 venue/deployer.

## What it is

Felix is a DeFi app on Hyperliquid that offers stablecoin lending and borrowing, a CDP stablecoin (feUSD), 24/7 derivatives trading, and U.S. spot equities bought with USDC. Its lending markets accept collateral such as HYPE and HYPE liquid staking tokens. It is used by HYPE holders, stablecoin lenders, and traders seeking onchain exposure to equities and commodities.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Felix deploys and operates HIP-3 perp markets on Hyperliquid for equities, commodities, and indices, and runs its lending and CDP products on HyperEVM.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-3 market metadata
- oracle and mark prices
- order book stream
- open interest per market
- liquidation feed
- HyperEVM events for lending and CDP positions
