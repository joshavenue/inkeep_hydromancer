# Ventuals

Ventuals is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: HIP-3 venue/deployer.

## What it is

Ventuals offered 24/7 perpetual futures on private companies such as SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic, plus thematic baskets. It ran a vHYPE staking product and a liquidity provider vault. On June 15, 2026 the team announced it was winding down, settling and halting all its markets, and discontinuing its points program.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Ventuals deployed HIP-3 markets on Hyperliquid, which were settled and halted as part of its shutdown, with vHYPE redeemable for HYPE.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-3 market metadata
- oracle and mark prices
- open interest per market
- market settlement status
- staking balances for vHYPE

## Status

appears inactive or shut down (public evidence: app.ventuals.com post dated June 15, 2026 titled 'Ventuals is sunsetting' announcing wind-down and settlement of all its HIP-3 markets)
