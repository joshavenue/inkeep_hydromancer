# Share

Share is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: prediction markets.

## What it is

Share is a prediction market trading app on web and iOS that compares prices and routes orders across Polymarket and Hyperliquid. It includes sports data, social features such as following traders and trade alerts, and a points program. It is used by prediction market traders, including sports bettors.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

It describes itself as the trading app for Hyperliquid HIP-4 prediction markets and routes orders across HIP-4 deployers.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- HIP-4 outcome market metadata
- order book stream for outcome markets
- fills history per wallet
- live positions for many wallets
- recent trades stream
