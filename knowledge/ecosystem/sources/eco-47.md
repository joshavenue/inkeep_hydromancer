# Onyx

Onyx is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading terminal.

## What it is

Onyx is a trading terminal whose X bio says it gives users more edge and is powered by Hyperliquid. It is linked to the team behind Sentiment.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Onyx describes itself on X as a trading terminal powered by Hyperliquid.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- live positions per wallet
- fills history per wallet
- funding rates

## Status

appears inactive or shut down (public evidence: onyx.live shows 'This deployment is temporarily paused', and the most recent X post found dates from February 2025)
