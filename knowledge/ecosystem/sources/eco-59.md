# Stride

Stride is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Stride is a liquid staking protocol for Cosmos ecosystem chains that issues stTokens such as stATOM and stTIA. Stride governance voted to wind down the chain, and liquid staking and new redemptions closed on Oct 12, 2026. stTokens are to become redeemable on Osmosis at a fixed rate by Nov 20, 2026.
