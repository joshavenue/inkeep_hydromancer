# BharatLiquid

BharatLiquid is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: trading app/frontend.

## What it is

BharatLiquid (BL) is a trading app for users in India that offers exposure to global equities, crypto, commodities, and pre-IPO markets with rupee deposits and withdrawals. It is being built with Privy wallet infrastructure. It is currently in an early access waitlist phase, and pricing is to be published before trading opens.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Its site states it is built on Hyperliquid, connects users to Hyperliquid order books, and shows live market data from the Hyperliquid websocket API.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- order book stream
- candles
- recent trades stream
- mark prices
- HIP-3 market metadata
- fills history per wallet
