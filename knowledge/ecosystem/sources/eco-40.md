# Liminal

Liminal is a Hyperliquid ecosystem app. Category: other.

## What it is

Liminal is a yield protocol that runs delta-neutral strategies on Hyperliquid, mainly capturing perpetual funding rates while hedging price exposure. Users can mint tokenized strategies such as limUSD and xTokens (for example xHYPE and xBTC) or design custom self-custodial strategies. Its users are yield-focused DeFi depositors.

## How it relates to Hyperliquid

Liminal executes spot and perp hedges on HyperCore to harvest Hyperliquid funding rates, uses HyperEVM for composable yield tokens, and is expanding into HIP-3 markets.

## Hyperliquid data an app like this needs

- funding rates (live and historical)
- spot and perp order books
- account margin and positions for strategy vaults
- HIP-3 market metadata
- fills history per vault
